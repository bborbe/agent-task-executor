# Split the re-drive cap by failure class: retry the transient, close the broken

<summary>
- The cap stops treating every three-times-failed record the same way
- A record whose three attempts failed with the same broken signature is closed `aborted`, with a machine-readable reason on the task
- A record whose failures differ between attempts, or whose failure is transient, is re-driven under a bounded budget instead of being abandoned at three
- The executor gains the terminal write it does not have today: `status: aborted` plus the close-out pair vault-cli requires
- Each attempt's failure reason is persisted next to the streak, so "same signature on all three" is a comparison the store can answer rather than a guess
- The existing cap metric keeps its meaning — every refused spawn still increments it
- The classifier runs on the ordinary path, not only after a restart
- A record already capped before this change carries no history, so it is refused as before and closed by nothing — the operator's un-cap path (delete its refs) is unchanged
- No record is closed by hand and no record is abandoned without a recorded reason
</summary>

<objective>
Give the consecutive-re-drive cap a decision instead of a wall. Today the fourth spawn at a phase is refused forever and the record stays `in_progress` with no reason recorded. Split that single behaviour in two: a broken signature closes terminally with a reason, a transient failure gets a bounded extension and then closes with its own reason.
</objective>
